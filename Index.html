01 · About

A life in progress.

Albert Mutale is a university student with an interest in science, engineering, problem-solving and continuous personal development.

This website is a professional introduction and a growing record of his academic interests, reflections, experiences and ambitions. It is not intended to present a finished story, but to document the process of learning, making decisions and moving forward with purpose.
